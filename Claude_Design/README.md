## Design Philosophy

FretWise should feel **musical, precise, and approachable**.

- **Dark-first**: A dark theme reduces eye strain during long practice sessions.
- **Guitar-centric visuals**: The fretboard and notation are the heroes of every view.
- **Progressive disclosure**: Simple controls are visible by default; advanced options appear on demand.
- **Responsive panels**: All panels are resizable so players can zoom the fretboard or the score independently.
