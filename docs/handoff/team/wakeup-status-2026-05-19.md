(`test_canonical_to_render_scene_tablature_rhythm_anchors_time_signature_on_tab_plane`
and `test_canonical_to_render_scene_standard_tab_uses_tab_letters_without_string_names`)
both assert no string_label instances. Fixed by always passing `open_pitches=None`.

resolved**.
